How it works

The network is built in three stages, in order. Each one funds the next.

I

UNDERSTANDING

World news, how power and capital actually move, and how countries compare for where to live, bank and build.

II

MONEY

Online ventures, running operations and trading. Money is the tool, not the goal: enough to live well, with a surplus to spend on something bigger.

III

ACTION

Members propose projects with real budgets and locations. The group votes, funds and runs them together.
